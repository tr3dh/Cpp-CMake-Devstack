GMP LGPLv3 Relink Instructions (MSYS2 MinGW)
============================================

Source Code: gmp-6.3.0.tar.xz (unverändert)

Provide Build : Mingw
--------------------------------
pacman -S mingw-w64-x86_64-gmp mingw-w64-x86_64-gmpxx

Provide Build : aus Source bauen
--------------------------
tar -xf gmp-6.3.0.tar.xz
cd gmp-6.3.0
./configure --enable-static --enable-cxx --host=x86_64-w64-mingw32
make -j$(nproc)
cp .libs/libgmp.a .libs/libgmpxx.a $MINGW_PREFIX/lib/

Dann Projekt neu bauen über: $ make launch
